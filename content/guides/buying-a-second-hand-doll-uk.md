Buying a doll second-hand used to mean taking a chance on a forum post and a stranger's bank details. It doesn't have to be like that now. A pre-owned doll can be a smart buy: someone else has already paid the new price, absorbed the import costs and waited six weeks for delivery. You get a doll that exists, is in the UK and can be checked before you commit.

This guide covers what you need to know before buying one: the law, hygiene, what to inspect and how to pay without getting burned.

## Is it legal to buy a pre-owned sex doll in the UK?

Yes. Adult sex dolls are legal to buy, own and resell in the UK, and pre-owned dolls are covered by the same rules as new ones.

The firm legal line is about **appearance**. A doll with a childlike appearance is treated very differently. The Crown Prosecution Service's guidance explains that importing, selling or posting one can be prosecuted under the Customs Consolidation Act 1876, the Obscene Publications Act 1959 and the Postal Services Act 2000. As of late 2026, a government-backed private member's bill, the Child-like Sexual Abuse Dolls (Offences) Bill, was also at committee stage. It would make possession an offence too, with a maximum sentence of five years.

What that means for you as a buyer:

- Only consider dolls that are clearly adult in face, body, styling and description.
- Be wary of any listing that plays down a doll's appearance with a claimed age or height. A responsible marketplace will refuse those listings, and so should you.
- At SellMyDoll we don't accept full-body dolls under 150 cm, and we check every listing by hand before it goes live. Our [prohibited items policy](/legal/prohibited-items) sets out the rules in full.

## Why buy second-hand at all?

**Price.** As a rough guide, a well-kept pre-owned doll sells for about 40–60% of what it cost new. On a doll that cost £1,500, that's a saving of £600 to £900.

**No waiting.** New dolls are often made to order and shipped from overseas. A pre-owned doll is already in the UK.

**No surprises.** With a new custom order, you only see the result when it arrives. With a pre-owned doll, you can see exactly what you're getting, including the face, skin tone, wig and how she holds a pose, from recent real photos.

**Less waste.** Dolls are heavy, resource-intensive products. Giving one a second home is better than it ending up in a skip.

## The honest truth about hygiene

This is the question most people are too embarrassed to ask, so here's a straight answer.

**Silicone is non-porous.** Its surface doesn't absorb fluids or bacteria, so a pre-owned silicone doll can be cleaned and sanitised thoroughly.

**TPE is porous.** It's softer and cheaper, but it has microscopic pores that can hold moisture and bacteria. No home cleaning method can promise to sterilise TPE completely. That's why it matters so much what kind of opening the doll has:

- **Removable inserts** can simply be replaced. A used insert should never be part of a sale. At SellMyDoll, sellers must either include a **new, sealed** insert or leave it out of the sale. Replacement inserts are cheap and easy to buy.
- **Fixed (built-in) openings** can't be replaced. On a TPE doll with a fixed opening, ask the seller directly how often it was used, how it was cleaned and dried, and whether there's any discolouration or smell. If the answers are vague, walk away.

Whatever you buy, deep-clean it when it arrives (our [cleaning guide](/guides/how-to-clean-a-pre-owned-doll) takes you through it step by step).

## What to check before you buy

Ask for **recent** photos, ideally taken in daylight and dated, or with a note showing the listing reference. Then work through this list.

### Skin

- Tears or splits, especially at the armpits, inner thighs, groin, fingers and neck seam
- Sticky or oily patches (common on TPE that hasn't been powdered)
- Stains, particularly dye marks from dark clothing, which are usually permanent on TPE
- Flat spots on the buttocks or back from long periods lying in a box

### Skeleton

- Does she still hold a pose, or do the joints flop?
- Any clicking, grinding or metal showing through the skin?
- Are the shoulders and hips symmetrical when standing or sitting?

A short video of the doll being gently posed tells you more than ten photos.

### Head and face

- Is the makeup intact? Repainting is possible but costs money.
- Does the neck bolt fit firmly, without wobbling?
- Are the eyes clear and seated properly?

### Paperwork and accessories

- The original brand and model, so you can check the new price and specification
- Spare wig, clothing, standing feet and a cleaning kit all add value
- The original box makes delivery and storage far easier

## How to pay without getting scammed

The biggest risk in private doll sales isn't the doll. It's the payment. Common scams include sellers who ask for a bank transfer, then disappear, and "couriers" who need an extra fee before delivery.

- **Don't pay a stranger by bank transfer** for a doll you haven't seen and can't have collected.
- **Keep the conversation on the platform** you found the doll on, so there's a record if something goes wrong.
- **Use a brokered sale where you can.** On a SellMyDoll brokered sale, we screen both sides and confirm payment and handover arrangements, so neither of you is taking a leap of faith.

## Delivery and discretion

A full-size doll in a box usually weighs 30–50 kg and is around 1.5 m long, so it isn't something you can carry home on the bus. Your options are usually:

1. **Collection**, which is fine if you're local and comfortable meeting.
2. **Seller-arranged courier**: ask how it will be packed and labelled.
3. **Our delivery package**: collected and delivered in an unmarked box, with nothing on the label describing the contents, anywhere in mainland UK.

For most buyers, yes. That applies if you buy from a seller who has looked after the doll, you choose silicone or a TPE doll with a removable insert, and you pay through a route that protects you. You'll get a better doll for your money than buying new at the same budget.
