A lot of people worry about cleaning before they've even bought a doll. It sounds like a big job. In practice, once you've done it a couple of times it takes ten minutes, and it's the single biggest thing you can do to keep a doll looking good for years. It's also exactly what the next owner will ask about if you ever decide to sell.

## What you'll need

- Mild, fragrance-free, pH-neutral soap (or a dedicated doll cleaner)
- Warm water, around body temperature
- An irrigation bottle or cleaning kit for internal areas
- A soft, **white or colourless** towel (coloured towels can transfer dye)
- Drying sticks
- Talc-free renewal powder or cornflour, plus a soft make-up brush
- Optional: a UV sterilising and heating rod

## Everyday cleaning, step by step

1. **Lay her on a stable, padded surface**, such as a towel on the bed. Don't try to hold her upright in the shower.
2. **Clean intimate areas first.** Fill the irrigation bottle with warm, mildly soapy water and flush the area two or three times, then repeat with clean water. With a removable insert, take it out and wash it separately. That's much easier and more thorough.
3. **Wipe the body** with a soft cloth and diluted soap, working from top to bottom. Pay attention to the creases at the neck, armpits and thighs.
4. **Rinse** with a cloth dampened in clean warm water.
5. **Pat dry. Don't rub.** Rubbing TPE can roughen the surface and cause small tears.
6. **Dry internal areas** with a drying stick for 15–30 minutes, and repeat if it's still damp. Don't leave drying sticks in long-term.
7. **Powder TPE skin** with a light, even coat brushed on with a soft brush. This absorbs oil and stops the skin feeling tacky. Silicone needs much less powder, if any.

## Deep-cleaning a second-hand doll when she arrives

You don't know exactly how she was stored on the way to you, so give her a full reset before anything else:

1. **Unbox her carefully** and check her against the listing photos straight away, before you clean her. If anything doesn't match, photograph it.
2. **Bin any used insert.** A removable insert should be new and sealed. If it isn't, replace it. They're inexpensive.
3. **Do a full body wash** as above, and take your time over creases and seams.
4. **Flush fixed openings several times**, then dry them thoroughly with repeated drying sticks.
5. **Optionally**, use a UV sterilising rod on internal areas.
6. **Powder and leave her to air-dry for 24 hours**, lying on a clean towel or hanging, before storing or using her.

## Cleaning the head and face

The head needs the most care, because it's where the makeup and the metal neck bolt are.

- **Never soak or rinse the head under a tap.** Water in the neck can rust the bolt, and it lifts painted makeup.
- **Spot-clean only**, using a barely damp cloth with a little soap, and avoid the eyes, lips and brows.
- **Wash wigs separately** in cool water with a little wig shampoo or gentle conditioner, then let them dry on a stand.

## Dolls with electronics

If your doll has heating, sound, movement or any other electronics, **don't shower or submerge her body**. Spot-clean with a damp cloth and keep water well away from charging ports and control units. Check the manufacturer's instructions, and if you're selling, include them in your listing.

## The mistakes that cause permanent damage

- **Hot water.** It can deform TPE and loosen the skin from the skeleton.
- **Alcohol, bleach or harsh antibacterials.** These dry out, discolour and crack the material.
- **Oil-based products on TPE**, including baby oil and many makeup removers.
- **Coloured towels and dark clothing on damp skin.** Dye transfer to TPE is usually permanent.
- **Storing her damp.** Trapped moisture is how mould starts, especially in internal areas.
- **Scrubbing.** Gentle wins, every time.

## Why cleaning matters when you sell

When you come to sell, buyers will ask how she's been looked after. "Cleaned and powdered after every use, insert replaced every few months, stored dry" is one of the most valuable sentences you can put in a listing. Take a photo of your cleaning kit and include it. Small things like that build trust (see [how to list your doll so it sells](/guides/how-to-photograph-and-list-your-doll)).
